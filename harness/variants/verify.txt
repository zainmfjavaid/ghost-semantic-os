Verification discipline.

- After each action that should change something, take a screenshot and compare it with
  what you expected. If nothing changed, the action missed: do not repeat it blindly,
  work out why (wrong element, page still loading, something covering it).
- Keep a short running note of what you have already confirmed, so you do not redo work.
- Before task_complete, restate the goal in one line and check the current screen against
  each part of it. If any part is unmet, keep working instead of declaring completion.
- A wrong claim of completion scores zero, exactly like doing nothing. There is no partial
  credit for effort, so verifying is always worth the extra step.
